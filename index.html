The story

A host, not a tour operator.

I built Adequate Safaris because the trips I wanted didn't exist yet. Every year I pick a place, work out the details, and take the people who feel like family with me. We move slowly, eat well, and end most days watching the light go. Thirty-odd countries later, the part I keep coming back for isn't the view. It's who is standing next to me when the sun drops.
